The platform · Clausehound

One engine for institutional knowledge

Clausehound turns scattered documents into a structured, searchable, comparable knowledge base — the foundation under every Cobalt service. A zero-copy system keeps your originals intact while metadata does the work.

Gather. Parse & structure

Document parsing, taxonomy mapping, and text clustering turn raw files into tagged, filterable data.

Research. Search & compare

An AI workbench with annotation and vector-based comparison locates the right clause across massive volumes.

Recommend. Draft from precedent

Multi-layered AI prompting draws drafting suggestions from your own institutional knowledge, with quality control built in.

Collaborate. Standardize the work

Workflow tracking, metadata capture, and playbooks keep a team drafting to one standard.
