Two ways to add due-diligence capability. One is a platform you license and staff yourself; the other is a tailored, results-based engagement with no fixed cost. Here is how they differ where it matters commercially.

You get the finished work

Scoped per matter, vetted by our research team, delivered ready to drop into the firm's draft.

You build the capability
