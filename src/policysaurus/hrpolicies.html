Human Resources Policies

Policysaurus’s Policysaurus product, along with their teams of lawyers, subject matter experts, and researchers, provides tools to manage research for the purpose of policy review, analysis, and development.

Policysaurus’s Policysaurus software will aid in the efficient and semi-automatic review of the your organizations’ documents, which will allow our subject matter experts to dedicate their time to addressing issues related to racism and discrimination in your policies.

Policysaurus’s unique data-driven approach to understanding legal documents for the provision of materials and training on many topics, including accessibility, anti-harassment, hiring, and other policy and legal documents.
