Rajah Lehal

Founder, Senior Lawyer, Chief Knowledge Officer

Rajah is a legal technologist, and a technology and M&A lawyer.

Prior to founding Cobalt Lawyers, Rajah received his legal training in the corporate law group at Stikeman Elliott LLP in Toronto, as well as internationally for the Technology Media and Telecom group in the Dubai office of Clyde & Co. His particular focus on technology and small business comes from a passion in business and entrepreneurship. A serial entrepreneur himself, Rajah is the visionary behind products that solve many of the difficult elements of document research including the Diligence Monster, Dealprep and Policysaurus products that are widely used by our team and clients. Cobalt Lawyers added the moniker Cobalt AI in 2023 with the launch of AI abilities in the Clausehound software.

Rajah received his M.B.A. and J.D. degrees, from the University of Western Ontario Richard Ivey School of Business and the Faculty of Law respectively, during which time he was Co-President of Entrepreneurs@Ivey, a student-run entrepreneurship group, and the inaugural Student Director of the Western Business Law Clinic. Prior to that, Rajah attended the University of Waterloo and worked for more than a decade in the information technology industry, including three years as a software developer, and seven years as an Information Systems and Technology manager at both a major telecommunications company and at a financial services company.

Josh Koudys

Chief Technology Officer

Josh is the engineer our lawyers call when a pile of documents needs to become data. He has been building Clausehound since 2016, and has been its CTO since 2018: the clause taxonomy at its core, the versioned corpus that keeps every amendment to the laws we track, and the connections that let clients point their own AI agents straight at it.

He started out writing C inside IBM's DB2 database engine, where he helped build its row compression, then spent five years at IBM keeping enterprise commerce systems standing through peak season. In 2011 he founded Qaribou, a consultancy doing fractional CTO work before anyone called it that: geodata platforms (including the maps behind Jane's Walk, used by more than 500,000 walkers), document processing, fintech analytics, and a lot of careful surgery on legacy systems nobody else wanted to touch.

Josh is a co-inventor, with Rajah Lehal and Gregory O'Grady, of US Pat. No. 12,670,200, Clause Taxonomy System and Method for Structured Document Construction and Analysis. He holds a B.Eng. from Western, has taught full-stack development at Lighthouse Labs and George Brown College, and his open-source PHP library has been installed about 150,000 times. His rule for AI in legal work is short: every result tied to its source, every change on the record, and a person accountable for what ships.
