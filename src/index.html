Why us

Software got cheap. Judgment didn't.

AI can draft a contract in seconds, and be confidently wrong in the same breath. The value now is in knowing which answer is right, and standing behind it. Our platform does the reading at machine speed. Our lawyers and researchers check every deliverable before it reaches your draft.

What the AI does

  • Parses and structures thousands of documents
  • Finds and compares clauses across your precedent
  • Drafts extracts, schedules and suggested language

What our people do

  • Scope the matter with you, and agree the fee
  • Verify every finding against its source
  • Sign off the deliverable, ready for your draft
Current, cited, on the record

The modern, boring, thorough lawyer.

We're real, human lawyers and researchers. Every law and regulation in our platform is kept under full change control: each amendment is recorded, dated and kept, never overwritten. So we can answer the question most AI tools can't: what did the law say then, what does it say now, and which provisions changed in between.

  • Point in time. Ask what changed for your business between any two dates, and get the answer provision by provision.
  • No citation, no claim. Every finding is tied to the source text it came from. If we can't cite it, we don't say it.
  • Kept current by people. Our lawyers track amendments as they're made, and review what the platform ingests.

A model that starts at zero.

No licences, no seats, no committed spend before the work is real.

The platform

Built on Clausehound, our patented knowledge engine.

Clausehound turns scattered documents into a structured, searchable, comparable knowledge base. Originals stay intact; metadata does the work. It's what lets a small team review like a large one. Its clause taxonomy, versioning and analysis are protected by our patent, Clause Taxonomy System and Method for Structured Document Construction and Analysis, US Pat. No. 12,670,200.

  1. Gather

    Parsing, taxonomy mapping and text clustering turn raw files into tagged, filterable data.

  2. Research

    An AI workbench with annotation and vector comparison finds the right clause across massive volumes.

  3. Recommend

    Drafting suggestions drawn from your own institutional knowledge, with quality control built in.

  4. Collaborate

    Workflow tracking, metadata capture and playbooks keep a team drafting to one standard.
